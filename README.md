# MarketMind
### Smarter Marketing. Stronger Businesses. Greater Opportunities.

MarketMind is a Business Growth Intelligence platform designed to help small businesses and entrepreneurs make smarter marketing decisions through structured assessments, practical recommendations, and actionable growth plans.

## The Problem

Many small businesses struggle to develop effective marketing strategies because they lack access to structured marketing guidance, resources, and strategic planning tools.

Without a clear understanding of their marketing strengths and weaknesses, business owners may struggle to identify opportunities, reach the right customers, and plan sustainable growth.

## Our Solution

MarketMind provides a structured digital workspace that helps businesses understand their current marketing position and turn challenges into actionable growth decisions.

The platform uses rules-based assessment logic to evaluate business information and generate practical recommendations and planning tools.

## Key Features

- **Business Profile:** Create and save a structured business profile.
- **Growth Assessment:** Evaluate marketing readiness and identify potential gaps.
- **Strategy Builder:** Generate structured marketing strategy recommendations.
- **Campaign Studio:** Develop focused marketing campaign plans.
- **90-Day Growth Planner:** Create a phased roadmap with tasks and key performance indicators.
- **Progress Tracking:** Track completed growth-planning tasks and monitor progress.

## How It Works

1. Enter your business information.
2. Complete the business growth assessment.
3. Review identified marketing gaps and opportunities.
4. Generate strategic recommendations and campaign plans.
5. Follow your 90-day roadmap and track completed tasks.

MarketMind aims to make structured marketing guidance more accessible to small businesses and resource-constrained entrepreneurs.

By simplifying marketing assessment and planning, the platform encourages informed decision-making, stronger business strategies, and more sustainable growth opportunities.
